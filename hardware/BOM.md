# Prototype Bill of Materials

## M0.1 — one pad

| Qty | Part | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | ESP32-C3 SuperMini | Existing controller is suitable for prototype |
| 1 | 27–35 mm piezo disc | Impact/vibration sensor |
| 1 | 1 MΩ resistor | Piezo discharge/shunt |
| 1 | 47 kΩ resistor | Series current limiting |
| 2 | Schottky diodes or suitable dual device | External rail clamping |
| 1 | Breadboard / perfboard | Prototype input circuit |
| 1 | ~8-inch rigid plate | Plywood/acrylic/other test plate |
| 1 | Rubber/foam playing layer | Stick surface |
| as needed | Foam isolation | Decouple pad from base/frame |
| as needed | Wire/connectors | Keep leads mechanically secure |

## Later expansion

| Qty | Part | Use |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | MCP3008 | 8-channel SPI ADC |
| 7+ | Additional piezo discs | Extra pads/zones |
| 1 | Hall-effect sensor | Hi-hat pedal position |
| 1 | Small magnet | Hi-hat pedal position |
| optional | 5-pin DIN MIDI components | Wired MIDI output |

No final PCB or enclosure components should be purchased until the prototype gates justify them.
